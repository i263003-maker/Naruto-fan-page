About & Plot Summary

About: Naruto is one of the most iconic anime and manga franchises in world history. Created by Masashi Kishimoto, the saga spans original Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, capturing the evolution of ninja culture, bonds, and perseverance.

Plot Summary: The story follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja shunned by his village due to the Nine-Tails sealed inside him. Vowing to become the Hokage to earn respect, Naruto builds deep rivalries, masters legendary jutsu, and protects the world from the Akatsuki.

Main Protagonists

Naruto Uzumaki

Role: Main Protagonist

A stubborn, energetic ninja from Team 7 who harbors the Nine-Tails. He masters Sage Mode and Rasengan on his journey to become the 7th Hokage.

Sasuke Uchiha

Role: Rival & Co-Protagonist

A prodigy of the Uchiha clan who possesses the Sharingan and Rinnegan. Driven by vengeance, he seeks his own path of ninja justice.

Sakura Haruno

Role: Medical Shinobi

A talented kunoichi of Team 7 who trains under Tsunade to become a master medical ninja with superhuman strength.

Kakashi Hatake

Role: Team Leader & Mentor

Famous as the "Copy Ninja," Kakashi guides Team 7 with wisdom, his Sharingan, and signature Lightning Blade (Chidori).
